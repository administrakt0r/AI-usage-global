## Anthropic Signs $517B in Compute Leases Totaling 14.8 GW

### Analysis reveals massive 11-month infrastructure expansion driven by hyperscale cloud and data center agreements.

AI lab Anthropic has committed to an estimated $517 billion in compute capacity leases over the past 11 months, locking in 14.8 gigawatts of power and server capacity. The rapid expansion highlights how leading AI developers are securing utility-scale energy and hardware commitments years in advance.

## Key details

According to an analysis compiled by The Information and reported by Data Center Dynamics, Anthropic's compute commitments have escalated dramatically since October. The company secured 14.8 gigawatts (GW) of compute capacity through public agreements and private deals. This builds on an initial base of 1 GW to 2 GW of capacity previously held by the AI developer.

Hyperscale cloud partners represent the majority of this power footprint. Google and Amazon Web Services (AWS) account for 11 GW of the total 14.8 GW capacity. Major commitments include a $200 billion deal with Google for custom TPU capacity, a massive cluster of AWS custom Trainium hardware, a $50 billion partnership with AI cloud operator Fluidstack, and a $45 billion compute agreement with Nscale.

In addition to hyperscalers, Anthropic signed letters of intent with multiple US data center developers. Key regional deployments include a 191 megawatt (MW) lease with Riot Platforms in Texas, as well as compute agreements with Akamai, CoreWeave, SpaceX/xAI, Lambda, Volta Infra, AMD, and Microsoft. While Anthropic previously projected renting $180 billion in server capacity through 2029, its active commitments have already surpassed $517 billion.

### Why this matters

The $517 billion commitment demonstrates how AI scale-up costs are driving record-breaking capital allocations into physical energy and data center infrastructure. Securing 14.8 GW of capacity requires power equivalent to the generation capacity of several major metropolitan utility grids, intensifying competition for transmission interconnections, electrical equipment, and power generation.

### Context

Hyperscalers and frontier AI labs are shifting toward multi-gigawatt compute leases to ensure hardware availability amid tight supply chains and power grid constraints. Anthropic's $517 billion compute portfolio mirrors industry-wide capex increases by competitors like OpenAI and Meta, where long-term power and server availability have replaced algorithm design as the primary constraint on model scaling.

## Risks and open questions

A primary risk is whether regional electrical grids and utility suppliers can deliver 14.8 GW of energized capacity within requested timelines without triggering rate hikes or local grid stability issues. Additionally, financing hundreds of billions of dollars in long-term compute debt places immense financial reliance on sustained enterprise AI revenue growth.

## What happens next

Anthropic will continue working with cloud providers and data center developers to bring new facilities online. Regulatory scrutiny over utility rate impacts and grid interconnection queues is expected to increase as these gigawatt-scale projects submit formal interconnection requests.
