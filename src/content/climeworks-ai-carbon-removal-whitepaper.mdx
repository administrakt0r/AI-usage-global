## Climeworks Urges Carbon Removal Inclusion in Rapid AI Infrastructure Expansion

### New whitepaper outlines strategic pathways for data centers and hyperscalers to mitigate unavoidable emissions.

Climeworks Solutions published a whitepaper on August 26, 2026, calling on tech companies, data center developers, and energy suppliers to incorporate carbon removal into early-stage AI infrastructure planning. As digital infrastructure rapid buildouts drive surging demand for electricity and construction materials, the company emphasizes that emissions mitigation must extend beyond efficiency gains and clean energy contracts.

## Key details

The whitepaper, titled *Responsible AI Infrastructure Should Include Carbon Removal*, addresses the environmental footprint created by the global surge in AI data center construction. Rapid expansion requires substantial investments in computing hardware, energy infrastructure, and carbon-intensive materials like steel and concrete. Even with aggressive clean energy procurement and efficiency improvements, residual emissions during construction and operations remain difficult to eliminate entirely.

To prevent carbon mitigation from becoming an afterthought, Climeworks Solutions outlines four practical strategies for companies across the AI value chain:

- **Regional Impact Portfolios:** Supporting localized carbon removal projects selected for their specific environmental, economic, and community benefits in regions where data centers operate.
- **Ecosystem Co-Procurement:** Establishing shared responsibility for connected emissions by aggregating carbon removal demand across hyperscalers, developers, utilities, hardware suppliers, and tenants.
- **Natural Gas Transition Guardrails:** Combining carbon capture and durable carbon removal with explicit phase-out timelines when temporary natural gas power is used prior to clean energy integration.
- **Build-Stage Neutralization:** Integrating carbon removal procurement directly into initial project financing to neutralize embodied emissions from construction and hardware.

### Why this matters

The AI buildout is triggering unprecedented energy demand and physical infrastructure expansion worldwide. While hyperscalers have set ambitious zero-carbon goals, grid interconnection delays and reliance on fossil-fuel microgrids mean baseline carbon emissions continue to rise. Incorporating permanent carbon removal into the infrastructure financing phase provides a mechanism to compensate for hard-to-abate emissions throughout the hardware and building lifecycle.

### Context

This initiative comes as tech giants face scrutiny over the growing carbon footprint of AI model training and inference. Recent sustainability reports from major cloud providers show rising emissions tied directly to data center expansion and supply chain manufacturing. By introducing co-procurement frameworks and transition guardrails, the proposed strategies aim to hold developers and operators accountable before long-term infrastructure commitments lock in high-emissions trajectories.

## What happens next

Climeworks Solutions plans to convene key stakeholders from across the AI value chain during Climate Week NYC 2026 to discuss implementation frameworks and industry standard alignment. As regulatory pressure for transparent environmental reporting increases across the US and Europe, carbon removal integration could become a standard requirement for future AI infrastructure financing.
