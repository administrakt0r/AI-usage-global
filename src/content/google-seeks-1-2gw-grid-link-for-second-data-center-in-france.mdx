## Google Seeks 1.2GW Grid Link for Second Data Center in France

### Subsidiary Tricolore Computing filed for a 1,200 MW connection in Villedieu-sur-Indre as Google's nearby €8.6 billion Ozans campus enters public consultation.

Google subsidiary Tricolore Computing has requested a 1.2 GW grid connection in Villedieu-sur-Indre, central France, prompting grid operator RTE to outline a €67 million tie-in that would take seven to eight years to build, according to [Data Center Dynamics](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/google-planning-12gw-data-center-near-ch%C3%A2teauroux-france-report/). The 1,200 MW capacity request sits near Châteauroux, where Google is already consulting on a proposed €8.6 billion campus in Étrechet that is sized at 500 MW to 1.1 GW and undergoing public review.

## Key details

Citing investigative outlet Mediapart, Data Center Dynamics reported on November 25 that Google scouted several municipalities in the Indre department over recent months for a potential backup, or "mirror," facility to its Ozans Business Park project in Étrechet. French trade outlet [DCmag](https://dcmag.fr/google-a-etudie-un-projet-de-data-center-a-villedieu-sur-indre/) reported that Tricolore Computing submitted the 1,200 MW grid application in May 2026 and received an initial power reservation from RTE in early October.

Villedieu-sur-Indre Mayor Xavier Elbaz confirmed talks with Google, but told reporters the plans were placed on hold while the company focuses on Ozans. Elbaz also said the private landowner of the targeted parcel opposed selling to Google.

A Google spokesperson told Data Center Dynamics: "Google continuously explores land and grid opportunities to anticipate future infrastructure needs across the region. No final decision has been made on Ozans, or any other site, which are strictly part of ongoing, exploratory long-term planning rather than active parallel projects."

At the primary Ozans site, records from France's [Commission nationale du débat public (CNDP)](https://www.debatpublic.fr/data-center-ozans-chateauroux-de-google-et-son-raccordement-electrique-8996) list Google France alongside Tricolore Computing, Violet Computing, and RTE as project leads. That campus spans about 195 hectares for eight to ten data center buildings, two electrical substations, a 225 kV underground link targeted for 2028 to 2029, and two 400 kV overhead lines exceeding 10 km starting from 2031.

### By the numbers

- **1.2 GW**: Grid connection capacity requested by Tricolore Computing in Villedieu-sur-Indre.
- **€67 million**: Estimated cost of RTE's proposed Villedieu-sur-Indre grid connection, with a seven-to-eight-year build timeline.
- **€8.6 billion**: Estimated Ozans campus and Google-side grid cost in the CNDP filing, excluding servers and software.
- **€125 million**: RTE's separate grid connection cost for Ozans (€100 million at 400 kV, €25 million at 225 kV).
- **195 hectares**: Footprint of the proposed Ozans campus across eight to ten buildings.

### Why this matters

Even exploratory hyperscale requests tie up grid engineering bandwidth and force multi-year high-voltage planning in rural networks. Indre lies south of France's main Paris data center cluster, where grid capacity is tighter and land is scarcer, pushing developers toward provincial transmission corridors.

Adding a second 1,200 MW load in the same department would compound demand on local 225 kV and 400 kV lines. CNDP records already note that RTE is running a parallel consultation on an independent 400 kV transmission line between Eguzon and Marmagne in the same area.

### Context

Google currently operates no self-built data centers in France, relying on third-party facilities from Equinix, Digital Realty, and Global Switch alongside a sovereign cloud partnership with Thales. To address local electricity-price and grid-strain concerns at Ozans, [ENGIE signed a Memorandum of Understanding](https://en.newsroom.engie.com/news/engie-and-google-to-partner-on-energy-solutions-for-a-potential-data-center-project-near-chateauroux-france-28082-314df.html) with Google on November 13 to study new French renewable generation, battery storage, and free waste-heat recovery for local district heating.

France's central regions have drawn massive AI power proposals since last year, including SoftBank's previously reported [€75 billion plan for 5 GW of French AI capacity](https://aiusageglobal.pages.dev/blog-detail/softbank-france-5gw-ai-data-center).

## Risks and open questions

- **No site control or permits**: In Villedieu-sur-Indre, the landowner has refused to sell, and no permits, construction timeline, or final investment decision exist.
- **Unresolved water and cooling specs**: Neither the DCD report nor the CNDP summary page specifies annual water withdrawals or cooling technology for the Villedieu-sur-Indre proposal.
- **Ozans uncertainty**: Google's statement emphasizes that no final investment decision has been made on Ozans either, despite €58.5 million ($68 million) in reported land plans and ongoing public debate.

## What happens next

The CNDP public consultation for the Ozans campus and its 225 kV and 400 kV RTE connections runs through January 10, 2027. Any revival of the Villedieu-sur-Indre mirror site would require landowner consent, municipal planning reviews, and formal RTE grid contracts after that review concludes.
