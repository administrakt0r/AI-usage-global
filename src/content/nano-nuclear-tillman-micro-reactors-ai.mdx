## Nano Nuclear and Tillman Partner on Micro Reactors for AI Data Centers

### Non-binding deal targets up to 6 GW of micro nuclear capacity for US AI campuses by 2040

Advanced nuclear technology developer Nano Nuclear has entered into a non-binding strategic commercial framework agreement with data center developer Tillman Digital Gateway. The partnership aims to deploy Kronos micro nuclear reactors to deliver dedicated, off-grid power to planned AI industrial zones across the United States.

## Key details

Under the strategic agreement, Nano Nuclear is designated as Tillman Digital Gateway's anticipated preferred nuclear technology provider. The companies plan to collaborate across site evaluation, regulatory licensing, customer engagement, and reactor deployment. The partners are targeting 2 gigawatts (GW) of nuclear generation capacity by the mid-2030s and up to 6 GW or more by 2040.

The deployment will rely on Nano Nuclear's Kronos micro modular reactor, a stationary high-temperature gas-cooled reactor capable of producing 15 megawatts (MW) of electricity. The units are engineered for long-term deployment of up to 20 years without requiring refueling, offering continuous baseload electricity directly to high-density compute infrastructure. Tillman Global Holdings, the parent company of Tillman Digital Gateway, will integrate the micro reactors into its multi-state AI industrial zone expansion strategy.

### Why this matters

As hyperscale AI clusters scale toward multi-gigawatt power requirements, public electrical grids face unprecedented congestion and interconnection delays. By embedding 15 MW micro reactors directly into AI campus designs during initial site development, data center developers can secure dedicated baseload power without waiting for grid upgrades or exposing local residential ratepayers to infrastructure surcharge costs.

### Context

This deal represents Nano Nuclear's second agreement in the digital infrastructure sector following a prior memorandum of understanding with Super Micro. It reflects a growing industry-wide shift toward on-site nuclear generation for AI compute. Major technology companies, including Amazon, Google, Oracle, Switch, and Equinix, have signed agreements with advanced nuclear developers to meet surging energy demands with carbon-free power.

## What happens next

Nano Nuclear and Tillman Digital Gateway will begin initial site evaluation, environmental diligence, and regulatory filing plans. Future deployments remain contingent upon site-specific definitive agreements, customer power purchase commitments, project financing, and approval from the U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission.
