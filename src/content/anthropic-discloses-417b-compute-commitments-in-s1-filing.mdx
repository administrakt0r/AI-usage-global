## Anthropic Discloses $417B Compute Commitments in S-1 Filing

### SEC IPO paperwork reveals massive multi-year data center and cloud liabilities

Generative AI developer Anthropic has filed its S-1 IPO paperwork with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission, disclosing $417 billion in aggregate future minimum payment obligations for compute and data center capacity. The 335-page filing provides unprecedented financial visibility into the soaring infrastructure costs required to train and run frontier AI models.

## Key details

According to the SEC filing, Anthropic recorded $16.3 billion in revenue during the first half of 2026, representing a 1,300 percent increase year-over-year. However, the company's long-term financial commitments are dominated by mounting cloud compute and data center obligations spanning terms from under one year to 20 years.

As of December 2025, Anthropic had entered into lease and commercial agreements with aggregate future minimum payment obligations of approximately $417 billion. This total includes $265.6 billion in fixed or minimum guaranteed commitments under contracts with terms ranging up to 20 years. Additionally, the company estimated $125 billion to $150 billion in payments under expanded commercial agreements for infrastructure capacity slated for delivery between 2028 and 2031.

Specific financial structures highlighted in the filing include a 20-year data center lease for a facility yet to be constructed, a $500 million power purchase contract backing a data center provider, and a $2.5 billion escrow deposit placed in April 2026 for a compute lease agreement. Despite escalating expenditure, Anthropic reported improving compute efficiency, noting that quarterly revenue grew 1,368 percent year-over-year while compute and infrastructure expenses grew 300 percent.

### Why this matters

Anthropic's S-1 filing quantifies the immense financial capital required to sustain frontier AI development. Disclosing over $417 billion in long-term compute commitments demonstrates that leading AI laboratories are locking in multi-gigawatt power and server capacity decades into the future. These fixed obligations create immense pressure on hyperscalers, utility providers, and data center developers to guarantee continuous electrical power, land, and cooling hardware.

### Context

The disclosures come amid unprecedented global data center expansion driven by frontier model training and inference. To secure sufficient compute, Anthropic has forged multi-billion-dollar hosting agreements across multiple providers, including Amazon Web Services, Google Cloud, Microsoft, SpaceXAI, Fluidstack, Nscale, Akamai, and Lambda. The magnitude of these commitments reflects how AI scaling costs have transformed software startups into capital-intensive infrastructure entities dependent on massive energy grids and hardware supply chains.

## What happens next

Anthropic plans to list on the Nasdaq under the ticker symbol ANT. Investors and energy market analysts will monitor how the company executes on its $417 billion infrastructure backlog, particularly as data center developers face grid interconnection delays and rising power procurement costs. Subsequent SEC filings will provide further updates on actual capital deployment and power consumption across Anthropic's hosting partners.
