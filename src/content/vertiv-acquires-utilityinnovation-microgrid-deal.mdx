## Vertiv Acquires Microgrid Provider UtilityInnovation Group

### Acquisition expands behind-the-meter capabilities to speed up AI data center deployment

Digital infrastructure giant Vertiv has agreed to acquire microgrid solutions provider UtilityInnovation Group (UIG) in a strategic deal to help AI data center operators bypass power grid bottlenecks and accelerate deployment times.

## Key details

Under the agreement, Vertiv will acquire UIG to extend its power and cooling infrastructure upstream directly to utility interconnections and on-site generation sources. The acquisition equips Vertiv with specialized microgrid control systems, engineering capabilities, and behind-the-meter power architecture designed to integrate multi-megawatt AI data center facilities with dedicated generation assets.

As utility interconnection backlogs stretch for years across major data center hubs, hyperscalers and neocloud operators are increasingly turning to off-grid and microgrid solutions to bring high-density AI compute clusters online faster. Vertiv previously established a 1 MW microgrid demonstration facility at its Customer Experience Center in Delaware, Ohio, to test microgrid integration with data center power distribution systems.

### Why this matters

The acquisition addresses the primary constraint facing global AI expansion: speed to power. By integrating UIG's microgrid management and utility interconnection software directly into its data center infrastructure portfolio, Vertiv aims to provide coordinated power architecture from energy source to chip, reducing the time required to bring new AI capacity online.

### Context

Grid constraints have become the single largest bottleneck for hyperscale AI deployments worldwide, with utilities in the United States and Europe facing historic interconnection queues and capacity limits. To avoid multi-year delays, AI infrastructure developers are deploying microgrids, battery energy storage systems, and behind-the-meter generation, shifting the data center energy model away from traditional grid dependence.

## What happens next

Vertiv plans to integrate UIG's microgrid control technologies into its modular data center product line. As AI data center developers evaluate alternative energy strategies, integrated microgrid architectures are expected to see rapid adoption among operators building gigawatt-scale AI factories.
