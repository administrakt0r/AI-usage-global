## NetworkOcean Tests Floating Solar AI Data Center in San Francisco Bay

### San Francisco startup powers an Nvidia H100 GPU using a 20kW floating solar array at sea

San Francisco startup NetworkOcean has successfully operated an Nvidia H100 GPU powered by a floating 20kW solar array in San Francisco Bay. The trial marks the first time customer AI workloads have been run from a floating solar data center designed to bypass land and grid constraints.

## Key details

NetworkOcean deployed a 12-panel floating solar array connected to a shipboard container housing an Nvidia H100 GPU server during a pilot test in San Francisco Bay. The startup went from initial design to active water deployment in 53 days. NetworkOcean initially planned to build submerged underwater data centers but pivoted to floating solar systems after encountering Bay Area permitting challenges. The company aims to scale its off-grid floating compute platform to a 1MW array in 2027 and targets 1GW of ocean-based solar compute capacity by 2030.

### Why this matters

Connecting high-density AI hardware to land-based power grids often requires years of regulatory approvals, utility interconnection queues, and heavy infrastructure investments. By mounting compute nodes directly on floating solar infrastructure, off-grid deployments bypass traditional transmission bottlenecks and land acquisitions, offering an alternative pathway for scaling AI infrastructure without straining municipal electrical grids or local water supplies.

### Context

The AI boom has driven unprecedented demand for utility power, leading several states and municipalities to institute moratoriums or ratepayer protections. Companies are increasingly exploring alternative power arrangements—including behind-the-meter natural gas, geothermal PPAs, orbital satellites, and off-grid solar microgrids—to secure immediate power for expanding AI training and inference workloads. NetworkOcean’s offshore approach reflects a growing industry push toward off-grid infrastructure.

## What happens next

NetworkOcean plans to transition from its 20kW prototype to a 1MW commercial array by 2027. The company must navigate marine environmental regulations, saltwater corrosion defense, and storm durability tests before deploying modular ocean-based compute clusters at scale.
