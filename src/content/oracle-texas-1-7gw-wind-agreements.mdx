## Oracle Secures 1.7 GW of Wind Energy for Texas AI Campus

### Ten power purchase agreements across ERCOT will supply carbon-free electricity to Oracle's Abilene data center campus.

Oracle has signed ten wind energy supply agreements across Texas expected to deliver over 1.7 GW of carbon-free electricity to the Electric Reliability Council of Texas grid. The contracted power is dedicated to supporting operations at Oracle's flagship AI data center campus in Abilene. The massive procurement reflects how cloud providers are scrambling to secure utility-scale renewable power to match the soaring electricity consumption of hyperscale AI compute clusters.

## Key details

The agreements span ten wind energy projects supplied by major renewable energy developers, including Clearway Energy, Engie North America, RWE, and Scout Clean Energy. Under a virtual Power Purchase Agreement with RWE, Oracle will offtake 433 MW from three operating wind farms in West Texas: Panther Creek I (142 MW), Panther Creek II (115.5 MW), and Panther Creek III (repowered to 215 MW) across Sterling, Howard, and Glasscock counties.

In addition, Oracle signed an agreement with Engie North America to offtake 568 MW of wind energy across a portfolio of Texas wind resources. Executive Vice President Mahesh Thiagarajan stated that the procurement aligns with Oracle's corporate goal to match 100 percent of its AI data center electricity consumption with carbon-free energy by 2035.

### Why this matters

Gigawatt-scale AI workloads require continuous, massive electrical capacity that risks straining regional transmission networks and inflating local electricity prices. By entering into long-term virtual power purchase agreements, Oracle aims to offset its immense load on the ERCOT grid without imposing direct infrastructure costs on local Texas ratepayers.

### Context

This 1.7 GW procurement marks Oracle's second major clean energy initiative in September 2026, following a Request for Proposals to solicit 2 GW of solar, wind, and geothermal power for its Project Jupiter campus in New Mexico. As hyperscalers scale training clusters to hundreds of thousands of GPUs, securing guaranteed clean energy capacity has become a critical operational requirement alongside hardware acquisition.

## Risks and open questions

Because wind energy is inherently intermittent, virtual power purchase agreements offset total kilowatt-hour consumption on a annual or monthly basis rather than providing matching hourly baseload power. As Oracle's Abilene facility scales toward full compute capacity, balancing real-time grid demand against fluctuating wind output could still require grid backstops or on-site energy storage solutions.

## What happens next

Oracle will continue executing power off-take agreements to meet its 2035 carbon-free matching pledge across its expanding global AI infrastructure footprint. Regulators and ERCOT grid operators will monitor the integration of these contracted wind resources as Abilene's power draw scales up over the coming years.
