## Anthropic Inks $19 Billion Lease for TeraWulf's Kentucky AI Campus

### Landmark 20-year deal secures 401 megawatts of critical infrastructure

Anthropic has signed a 20-year lease agreement with TeraWulf for a purpose-built AI infrastructure campus in Hawesville, Kentucky. The deal is expected to generate approximately $19 billion in contracted revenue, marking a massive commitment to long-term compute capacity.

## Key details

The agreement secures approximately 401 megawatts (MW) of critical IT load at TeraWulf's "Justified Data" campus. The site, a former aluminum smelting facility, is being transformed into a high-density AI hub. Initial operations are expected to begin in the second half of 2027, with the campus reaching full capacity by early 2028.

Notably, the facility will utilize a closed-loop cooling system designed to dissipate heat from high-density server racks without drawing water from local reservoirs. This system recycles a cooling liquid—a mixture of water and propylene glycol—radiating heat into the atmosphere through fans rather than relying on evaporative cooling that consumes millions of gallons of water.

### Why this matters

This deal highlights the escalating value of "power-ready" land and infrastructure. As AI labs face a global "electricity wall," sites with existing high-voltage transmission lines and substantial power permits have become strategic assets. By locking in a 20-year lease, Anthropic is securing long-term operational stability against a backdrop of increasing grid constraints and rising energy costs.

### Context

The Hawesville site leverages the existing industrial power infrastructure of a former smelter, a trend seen elsewhere as data center developers repurpose "brownfield" sites to bypass years-long waits for new grid interconnections. TeraWulf, which began as a Bitcoin mining operation, has successfully pivoted to AI infrastructure, reflecting a broader industry shift toward more profitable, high-density compute workloads.

## What happens next

Development of the Justified Data campus will proceed in multiple phases, with construction focusing on the purpose-built infrastructure required for Anthropic's specific hardware needs. This deal may serve as a blueprint for other Bitcoin miners looking to monetize their power-dense sites by transitioning into AI "landlords" for major model developers.
