## Invitium Energy Secures 5 GW of Gas Turbines for PA AI Data Centers

### PPL-Blackstone joint venture targets massive behind-the-meter generation

A non-utility joint venture between PPL Corp. and Blackstone Infrastructure has secured reservation agreements for more than 5 gigawatts of gas turbines. The venture, named Invitium Energy, aims to construct on-site combined-cycle gas plants to power AI data centers, bypassing public power grid interconnection backlogs across Pennsylvania.

## Key details

Under the non-utility joint venture, in which PPL holds a 51% stake, Invitium Energy has secured physical sites across Pennsylvania capable of hosting up to 14 gigawatts of new generation. The agreements cover more than 5 gigawatts (GW) of combined-cycle gas turbines, representing a potential capital investment of up to $15 billion through 2032.

Additionally, the PJM Interconnection has accepted approximately 5 GW of Invitium’s proposed generation projects into its interconnection queue. While commercial income from these long-term energy supply services agreements is not anticipated until the turbines go online—potentially as early as 2031—Invitium is also evaluating near-term energy storage options, such as battery deployments.

Meanwhile, PPL Electric reported that its service territory's under-construction data center load surged to more than 6.5 GW during the second quarter, up from 5 GW in the previous quarter. The utility's advanced data center pipeline in Pennsylvania grew 12% to a massive 31.8 GW, including 11 GW of projects already backed by electric service agreements to shield residential ratepayers.

### Why this matters

The massive scale of Invitium's 5 GW turbine reservation illustrates how AI's unrelenting demand for electricity is forcing a dramatic shift toward off-grid, behind-the-meter natural gas generation. Bypassing public transmission lines allows hyperscalers to deploy high-density compute capacity years faster than standard grid interconnections allow, but locks in long-term fossil-fuel reliance.

### Context

This venture is a key milestone for PPL and Blackstone, who established Invitium last year specifically to build, own, and operate dedicated power plants for hyperscale AI facilities. As transmission networks like the 13-state PJM Interconnection struggle to integrate massive new loads, developers are increasingly turning to private generation agreements to guarantee energy supply.

## What happens next

Invitium Energy is actively negotiating with hyperscalers and expects to announce at least one major data center power supply contract by the end of 2026. State-level regulators and environmental groups are likely to heavily scrutinize the permits for the proposed 5 GW of combined-cycle gas plants, particularly in light of growing regional concerns over carbon emissions and local air quality.
